# Video style notes (for Citad3L's original clips)

These are short style notes written while studying the kind of card video the owner pointed to. No third-party screenshots, frames or media are stored in this repo, and nothing third-party is used on the site.

**Framing**
- 16:9 sources, cover-cropped into roughly 16:10 cards with a 16 px radius.
- A small dark translucent status pill sits over the top-left corner. The caption sits below the card.
- Muted autoplaying loops of about 10–12 s, each with a poster image.

**Looks that define "that type" of clip**
1. **Defense/geo-intel dashboard:** darkened satellite photo textures with a blue tint, thin white grid hairlines, glowing cyan, magenta and red tracking marks, radar sweeps along an arc, and fast zooms with motion blur. Blacks are crushed with navy shadows; colour stays low except the overlays.
2. **Photoreal still life:** shallow depth of field, soft practical light, slow push-in, subtle grain.
3. **Abstract scientific 3D:** translucent iridescent structures on a void, slow orbit.
4. **High-speed VFX:** slow-motion dissolves into particle trails.
5. **Control-room silhouette:** a person rim-lit by monitor glow, with bokeh.

Shared traits: realistic or cinematic (never flat vector), one subject per clip, slow camera moves, filmic contrast and grain, and no on-screen text.

**What Citad3L does with this:** original generated clips in one grade (deep navy/black, cyan and amber accents, occasional magenta/red HUD marks), with a luminous glass rook as the emblem. See `MEDIA.md`.
