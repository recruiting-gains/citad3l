# Citad3L Card Video Art Direction

## A

**Look bible.** Every clip is a dark, real, lens-shot world. Use a defense-intel night palette:

- **Base and shadows:** base black #07090c (never pure #000) and shadow navy #0a1424.
- **Cool range:** steel-blue mids #1e3a5f, signal cyan #3fd8ff and ice highlights #cfefff.
- **Warm accent:** one amber accent per frame, #ffb347.
- **Alerts:** magenta #c04a9a and alert red #ff3b3b, used only in tiny amounts for HUD alerts.

**Contrast and grade.** Blacks are crushed but toe-lifted to about 3% so cards read as framed media on #0b0b0b and not as holes. On white they read as strong dark tiles inside the 16px radius. Highlights roll off softly with gentle halation and bloom on emissive light only. Mids are slightly desaturated except for cyan and amber.

**Grain and lens.** Add fine 35mm grain (about 1%, applied in post so the encoder doesn't smear it). Shoot on a 50–85mm lens at f/1.4–2 with shallow depth of field, slight oval bokeh and mild vignette. No anamorphic flares, no chromatic fringing beyond a hint.

**Light.** One motivated key per clip, usually the subject's own glow, plus cool ambient fill and a single warm kicker. Practical light sources only.

**Motion and HUD.** Motion is slow (push-in, orbit or parallax) and always ends where it began. HUD elements are thin 1px-feel cyan lines, corner-bracket boxes, arcs, ticks and dots, never characters.

**Pill zone.** Keep the top-left 25% of every frame dark and calm so the status pill sits cleanly.

## C

Reject a frame or clip if it shows any of the following.

**Text and branding**
- Any glyphs, pseudo-letters, numerals, scribbles on screens or notebooks, or watermarks.
- Logo-like marks, including anything resembling the reference "H".

**Geometry and physics**
- A rook that warps, melts, gains extra crenellations or loses rigidity between frames.
- Robot arm joints that bend impossibly, or added or duplicated links.
- Gripper interpenetrating the part.

**Loop quality**
- Visible pop, jump or flicker at the seam. Scrub the last 10 and first 10 frames.
- Brightness drift between the start and end of the clip.
- Ping-pong "rewind" tells, such as steam or haze reversing.

**Grade**
- Pure #000 blocks, or banding in gradients and haze (check at 100% zoom after encoding).
- Off-palette hues: green, neon purple dominance, or saturated orange beyond a single accent.
- Busy or bright detail in the top-left 25%, which fights the pill.

**AI and render tells**
- Smeared textures, boiling noise, plastic CG sheen, or over-sharpened halos.
- Face or hand intrusions, especially in `everyday`.

**Crop**
- The subject is lost at 16:10 or 4:3 phone crops. Test at the object-positions listed in B.

**Consistency**
- Any clip noticeably warmer, brighter or grainier than its siblings. Review all five side by side on #ffffff and #0b0b0b.

## D

**Masters**
- Grok output is 720p for clips that use a last frame. Upscale only if it is clean, and add grain after upscaling.
- Trim to an exact loop length with no audio track.

**Delivery formats**
- Primary: MP4 H.264 High, yuv420p, CRF 23–26, `-movflags +faststart`, target ≤ 2.5 MB per clip.
- Optional: AV1/WebM at ≤ 1.2 MB, listed first in `<source>`.
- Strip audio with `-an`.

**Posters**
- AVIF/WebP at 1280×720, ≤ 80 KB, from the frame specified in each slot.
- Use as `poster=` and as the static fallback.

**Playback attributes**
- `muted playsinline loop autoplay preload="none"`.
- Play and pause with IntersectionObserver.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, show the poster only.
- On Save-Data or slow connections, show the poster only.

**Framing**
- Use `object-fit: cover` with these positions:

| Slot | object-position |
|---|---|
| rook | `50% 58%` |
| ai | `55% 50%` |
| robotics | `60% 50%` |
| cyber | `65% 50%` |
| everyday | `45% 55%` |

- For phones (≤ 480px), keep the same values. Below 360px, nudge `cyber` to `70% 50%` so the gate stays in frame.

**Overlay**
- Use a pill-legibility scrim only, with no bottom gradient (captions sit below the card): `background: radial-gradient(120% 90% at 0 0, rgba(0,0,0,.38), transparent 45%)`.
- Add an inner hairline `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,.06)` on dark pages and `rgba(0,0,0,.06)` on white, so the 16px-radius edge reads cleanly against both themes.
